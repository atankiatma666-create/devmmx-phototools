Reporting a problem with the compressor

These details help us find the cause quickly:

  • Your phone or computer model, and the browser and its version (in Chrome: menu, Settings, About Chrome).
  • The file type, size and dimensions shown by the tool.
  • The limit you chose and whether “Allow smaller dimensions” was on.
  • The exact message the tool showed.

Please do not send personal photos

Do not attach ID photos, documents or pictures of people. If a problem only happens with one image, try to reproduce it with a harmless picture, like a photo of a plant or a wall, and send that instead.

Emailing us shares your email address and message with us. The privacy page explains how we handle it.
